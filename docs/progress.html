My Progress

Your lab completion across the workshop

Reference

This page tracks the hands-on labs you've completed. A lab is marked complete when you click its Check my work button. Work through the labs in any order — this updates as you go, and completed labs also show a ✓ in the sidebar.

📝 How progress is stored

Your progress is saved in this browser only (local storage) — it isn't synced across devices or sign-ins, and clearing your browser data resets it. In a hosted deployment, this is where per-participant progress from the workshop backend would appear.
