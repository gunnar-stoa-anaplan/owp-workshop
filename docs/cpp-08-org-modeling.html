Org Modeling, RIF & Hiring Freeze

Scenario org changes — reduction in force and hiring freezes

CPP Preview

The CPP includes scenario org modeling for planning larger structural changes to the organization — specifically reduction-in-force (RIF) and hiring-freeze planning. Rather than editing positions one at a time, this is where you model the shape of the org under a change and see the effect before committing to it.

This segment builds on the capabilities covered earlier in the CPP. It uses the same plan actions you use to create and move positions, and it respects the same selective access — you model changes only against the orgs and positions you're allowed to see and touch. The org-modeling views are the natural place to plan a RIF or a hiring freeze because the position, cost, and org relationships are already connected there.
