Partner workspace, logins, and what to have ready

Getting Started Setup

What You Need to Do the Labs

The guided labs in this workshop run inside an Anaplan partner workspace with an OWP training tenant. You need access to that workspace so you can log in, open the app, and test things for yourself as you follow along. The read-through sections work without it, but the hands-on labs assume you can sign in.

Getting Logged In

During the workshop, the OWP Delivery Advisor set up selective access on the training tenant and handed out login IDs so partners could log in and test. If you were in the session, use the login ID you were issued. If you are working through this guide afterward, you will need the equivalent access arranged for you before you can complete the labs.

✅ Do — Confirm Your Access

Before you reach the first lab, confirm with your manager that you have access to an Anaplan partner workspace with an OWP training tenant, and that you have a login ID. If access hasn't been set up yet, arrange it now rather than during a lab.

📝 Not Sure Which Workspace or Login?

Specifics such as the tenant URL and your login ID are handed out per participant and aren't repeated here. If you don't have them, ask in the workshop channel — that's the fastest way to get the right workspace, the right access level, and a login sorted out.

Prerequisites

To get the most out of the labs, make sure you have:

  • Anaplan platform knowledge — familiarity with modules, lists, and model building. This is not an Anaplan 101 session.
  • An understanding of pre-built apps — what a pre-built Anaplan app is, and how delivering one differs from building a model from scratch.
  • Partner workspace access — a partner workspace with the OWP training tenant, plus a login ID, confirmed before you start the guided labs.
