Workshop Wrap-up

What to do next and how to keep learning

Reference

You've worked through the full OWP delivery-partner workshop. This page pulls the two pillars back together, restates what a delivery partner should be able to do, and points you at what to do next.

The two pillars

The workshop covered two things side by side:

Delivering OWP

The app-led delivery methodology — start with the pre-built app, load data first, set and configure, then extend only what's needed. The delivery lifecycle, data templates and readiness, extensions versus core changes, the timeline, and integration and architecture.

The OWP application

How the app actually works — Workforce Budgeting (WFB), the Continuous Position Plan (CPP), and the security model. Positions and workers, transfers, skills matching, user roles, and selective access.

What a delivery partner should be able to do

Coming out of this workshop, you should be able to:

  • Position OWP and app-led delivery — explain why you start with a working app and load data first, rather than building from a blank piece of paper.
  • Run a data-readiness conversation — walk a customer through the templates, identify what's mandatory versus optional, and set expectations that data is the long pole in the tent.
  • Walk the CPP — show the dynamic view of the org, create and edit positions, run transfers, and demonstrate skills matching.
  • Scope an extension without breaking the upgrade path — add rather than change, know when a core change needs an Anaplan design conversation, and set the 60/40 fit expectation.
  • Explain the security model — user roles, the page-security matrix, and how selective access cascades across orgs and positions.

Next steps

  • Watch for the promised follow-up materials — the security / selective-access documentation and walkthrough videos, and the full user-roles and page-security matrix. See the Resources & Downloads page.
  • Engage your regional Delivery Advisor — there's a Delivery Advisor for EMEA and one for APAC. They're your customer-facing advisory contact for where to build extensions, what's already on the roadmap, and any change that might threaten upgradeability.
  • Explore OWP as a foundation — think ahead to PRP, capacity planning, and demand planning. Once people, positions, skills, and performance are normalized, the same data feeds the rest of the planning stack.
💡 After the Workshop

The best next move is a real engagement. Take the app-led methodology and the data-readiness conversation into your next OWP opportunity, lean on your Delivery Advisor early, and revisit the Q&A from Sessions page whenever a question comes up on a live project — chances are it's already been answered.
