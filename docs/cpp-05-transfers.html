Position & Worker Transfers

Moving positions and people; backfill vs. close

CPP Walkthrough 15 min

The CPP handles two kinds of moves: transferring a position from one org to another, and transferring a worker into a different open position. Both are plan actions, both are scheduled to a future date, and both take effect when the model rolls to that month.

Position transfer — worked example: Benjamin Rose

Benjamin Rose currently sits at the company level. The goal is to transfer his position to another org on a set date. As you configure the move, the CPP shows the impact on both the source and destination org — the total positions on each side update so you can see what the transfer does before it happens.

You can also override the cost center. By default an org maps one-to-one to a cost center, but here the position transfers into an org while its cost aligns to "Field Sales EMEA" instead of the default "outside sales" cost center. This is a customer enhancement that deliberately breaks the one-to-one org-to-cost-center mapping.

To complete the transfer you enter a reason and set a schedule date. When the model rolls to that date, the position moves and actuals align as HCM data arrives.

Worker transfer — worked example: Yumiko

Yumiko is a senior hardware engineer, senior associate, based in London. To move her, you search all planned and open positions in the CPP filtered to "hardware". The search returns:

  • Open senior hardware engineer roles
  • A senior director of hardware engineering — which would require a move to Boston
  • An EA role in London as an alternative

This illustrates the internal-transfer cost savings story: rather than opening a fresh requisition, you place an existing worker into an open position. It also introduces the backfill vs. close flag on the position Yumiko is leaving.

📝 Backfill vs. close

When a worker transfers out, you decide what happens to the vacated position. In this example, in October the vacated position ID flips to open (backfill — the role will be refilled) or to closed (close — the role goes away). The flag is what determines whether the org keeps the headcount or gives it up.

Worker Transfer for Yumiko Mackenzie — target-position search and the Close Departing Position? flag.

Limitations

⚠ Know the boundaries before you demo

Transfers are one at a time — there is no mass transfer yet (logged as a future enhancement). There is no approval process on transfers; a transfer simply executes when the scheduled date hits (approvals on transfers were also logged as a good future enhancement). And transfers are scheduled at the month level, not the day level.
