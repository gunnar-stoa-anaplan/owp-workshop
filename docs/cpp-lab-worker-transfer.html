Move a worker into an open position — backfill or close

Exercise Overview

In this lab you move an existing worker into an open position — an internal transfer. You'll work with Yumiko Mackenzie, a senior hardware engineer (senior associate) in London. Because selective access governs everything a user sees, the positions you can transfer to are the open and planned positions you have access to in the CPP. The key decision is what happens to the position Yumiko leaves behind: backfill it, or close it.

💡 How to use this lab

Work through the steps in your training tenant with real sample data. After each step, read what changes in the Current vs Target Position panels — that side-by-side is how you sanity-check a transfer before you schedule it. Remember transfers are scheduled at the month level, not the day.

Guided Steps — Transfer Yumiko

Navigate to the CPP plan actions and open Worker Transfer for Yumiko Mackenzie.

Start from Yumiko's worker card or from the Plan actions menu, then choose the worker-transfer action. Selective access limits the target positions you'll see to the orgs and positions you have rights to.

  1. Open Worker Transfer for Yumiko and confirm her current position: senior hardware engineer, senior associate, London.
  2. Select a transfer period. Transfers schedule at the month level, so pick the month the move should take effect (for example, October).
  3. Search all planned and open positions in the CPP, filtered to "hardware". You should see open senior hardware engineer roles and a senior director of hardware engineering (which would require a move to Boston). An EA role in London is an alternative that keeps her in London.
  4. Pick a target position from the results.
  5. Enter a transfer reason.
  6. Set the Close Departing Position? flag — leave it unchecked to backfill Yumiko's vacated position (its ID flips to open), or check it to close that position (its ID flips to closed).
  7. Review the Current vs Target Position panels, then Schedule Transfer. When the model rolls to the transfer month, the move applies and actuals align as HCM data arrives.

Transfers run one at a time — there's no mass transfer yet, and no approval process on a transfer: it executes when the scheduled month hits. Both are logged as future enhancements, so don't look for an approval step here.

Quick check

Yumiko moves out of her senior hardware engineer position and you leave Close Departing Position? unchecked. What happens to the position she vacated?

Correct. Leaving Close Departing Position? unchecked backfills the role — when the model rolls to the transfer month, the vacated position ID flips to open so it can be filled again. Checking the flag instead flips it to closed.
Not quite. The right answer is B. Unchecked means backfill: the vacated position ID flips to open. You'd only get a closed position (answer C) by checking the flag — and the position is never deleted or dragged along with the worker.
Quick check

Why does an internal transfer of Yumiko into an open hardware role tell a better cost story than opening a brand-new requisition?

Correct. An internal transfer fills an open position with a worker you already have, so you avoid the recruiting cost and net-new headcount of a fresh req. The CPP surfaces that saving as you compare the Current vs Target panels — the worked example is exactly about internal-transfer cost savings.
Not quite. The right answer is C. The saving comes from filling an open role with existing headcount instead of recruiting new headcount. Transfers still respect selective access, still touch the plan and its costs, and don't automatically change the worker's pay.

Debrief Questions

  1. You chose to backfill Yumiko's vacated position rather than close it. What in the org's plan would make you choose the opposite — closing the position instead?
  2. The senior director of hardware engineering role would move Yumiko to Boston, while the EA role keeps her in London. How would you weigh the cost, skills fit, and location trade-offs when advising the manager?
  3. There is no approval step on a transfer — it just executes when the month hits. If a customer needs sign-off before a worker moves, how would you frame that as a future enhancement versus something to handle with selective access today?
  4. Transfers are one at a time and scheduled at the month level. What would you tell a customer who wants to move a whole team on a specific day?
