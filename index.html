Welcome

This lab guide accompanies the Operational Workforce Planning (OWP) Delivery Partner Workshop — a technical enablement program for Anaplan delivery practitioners who will implement, extend, and support the OWP application. OWP is a pre-built Anaplan app that plans the workforce at the position and employee level and closes the loop between finance budgeting, HR position planning, and talent acquisition.

The workshop has two halves. Delivering OWP covers the app-led delivery methodology — how you take a pre-built app from data load to a running, extended solution. The OWP Application tours what the app does: Workforce Budgeting (WFB), the Continuous Position Plan (CPP), and the security and selective-access model. Start with the Workshop Overview to understand the structure and objectives.

📝 Source & Status

This guide is built from the two OWP workshop recordings (delivery methodology and the CPP deep dive). A few segments referenced in the sessions but not captured on the recordings — the WFB budgeting walkthrough, the OWP architecture / PATH detail, and the org-modeling / RIF / hiring-freeze demo — are marked [PENDING SME input] where they appear. Confirm any product detail against the current app before using it with a customer.
